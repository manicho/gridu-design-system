# Product Identity: gridu

**Created**: 2026-06-29
**Status**: Final — validated against `quickstart.md` (all 5 required checks pass)
**Scope**: Conceptual/strategic identity only — no color, typography, component, or other
visual design decision belongs in this document (deferred to Features 02-06). Written from
scratch; does not reference or derive from `gridu-landing/docs/brand-guidelines.md`.

## Purpose

gridu exists so that a service-business owner never has to choose between doing the work
they're actually paid for and staying on top of their own schedule. Booking, confirming,
reminding, and rescheduling appointments happens automatically inside WhatsApp — the app
their clients already use — so an owner stops losing paid hours to manual back-and-forth and
stops losing booked revenue to no-shows and double-bookings.

## Values

- **Effortless setup** — Getting gridu running should feel like a short conversation, not an
  onboarding project. No app to install, no admin panel to learn before it's useful, no
  multi-step configuration wizard. In practice: a new feature or setting is only "done" when
  it can be explained and used in two sentences.
- **Always-on reliability** — The agenda runs whether or not the owner is watching it.
  Reminders go out on time, bookings don't silently fail, and the owner is told about
  problems immediately rather than discovering them later. In practice: every booking-
  affecting action either succeeds visibly or fails loudly — it never fails silently.
- **Respectful brevity** — Every message gridu sends, to the owner or to their client, takes
  the least amount of the reader's time needed to convey the point. In practice: confirmations
  state the outcome first, explanations are optional follow-ups, not prerequisites.
- **Local fit** — gridu is built around how Chilean service businesses actually run today
  (solo or near-solo operators, WhatsApp as the primary channel, cash and transfer payments
  alongside cards), not around assumptions imported from enterprise scheduling software. In
  practice: a feature that only makes sense for a multi-location franchise with dedicated
  reception staff doesn't belong in the product until that's who it's actually serving.

## Personality

If gridu were a person, it would be the one capable employee who quietly keeps the schedule
under control without needing to be managed. Specific behaviors:

- It confirms and moves on — it doesn't pad a successful booking with extra reassurance the
  owner didn't ask for.
- When something goes wrong, it says so plainly and gives the next step, rather than
  apologizing at length or hiding the failure behind vague language.
- It stays even-keeled under the moments that would stress a human assistant — a late
  cancellation, a double-booking attempt, a client rescheduling twice in one day — and
  handles each the same calm way every time.
- It is not trying to be the owner's friend, and it is not corporate or formal either. It
  talks the way a competent coworker would: direct, plain, and to the point.

## Target Persona

**The owner who runs the whole show.** A solo or near-solo service-business operator in
Chile — a hairdresser, a kinesiologist, a manicurist, a personal trainer — who personally
performs the service *and* personally manages the booking calendar, today via a mix of
WhatsApp messages and a notebook or spreadsheet.

- **Context**: Not technical, and not interested in becoming technical to run their
  business. Works long, hands-on hours; time spent managing the schedule is time not spent
  earning. A missed or double-booked slot is a direct, immediate revenue loss, not an
  abstract inconvenience.
- **Needs**: A way to stop manually juggling messages and a written calendar, without
  adopting a new tool that requires training, a dashboard to check constantly, or a change
  in how their clients already reach them (WhatsApp).
